AIME Workspace Privacy Policy

AIME Workspace is a personal-use integration operated by the owner of the hideotones GitHub account. It is not a public service.

Information accessed and purpose

With the owner's Google OAuth authorization, AIME Workspace accesses Gmail messages and metadata, calendar information, Drive files, spreadsheet content, and document content as needed to carry out the owner's requests. Authorized actions can include reading and organizing information, drafting or sending email, managing events, and creating or editing files. Sending messages or changing account content requires the owner's approval.

Processing and sharing

Hermes Agent runs on the owner's computer. Information needed to fulfill requests may be sent to the AI model provider configured in Hermes and to tools or services the owner chooses. This can include email text, document excerpts, calendar details, or other task context. These providers process information under their applicable terms and privacy policies. Content is also sent to intended recipients or collaborators when the owner approves sending or sharing it.

Google account data is not sold or used for advertising. The operator does not use Google account data to train generalized AI models.

Storage and security

OAuth credentials are stored locally on the owner's computer, not in this public website or its repository. Task information may also be retained in local conversation history, logs, memories, generated files, or services used for a task. Retention by external providers depends on their settings and policies. The owner is responsible for securing the computer and managing those settings.

Control, revocation, and deletion

The owner can revoke access through Google Account connections and delete local credentials and stored task information. Revoking access stops future authorized access but does not automatically delete previously stored information or copies already sent to recipients or providers.

Website hosting

This information website has no application analytics, advertising, or sign-in form. GitHub Pages hosts it and may process visitor technical information under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Google API user data

AIME Workspace's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Contact

For questions about this personal-use integration, contact its operator through the contact options listed on the hideotones GitHub profile.
