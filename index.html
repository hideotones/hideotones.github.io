AIME Workspace

A private, personal-use Google Workspace integration operated by the owner of the hideotones GitHub account.

AIME runs through Hermes Agent to help its owner manage email, calendar events, Drive files, spreadsheets, and documents. It is not a public service and does not offer registration or access to other users.

Google account access

The owner authorizes access through Google OAuth. Depending on the task, the integration can read and organize email, send messages, manage calendar events, and read, create, or edit Drive files, Google Sheets, and Google Docs. Sending messages or changing Google account content requires the owner's approval.

This website provides information only. It does not sign users in or collect Google credentials.
